Fly home

Good to know

  • Breakfast is included at the hotel every morning.
  • Subway: tap your own phone or card at the turnstile. Rides are free after 12 in a week.
  • Wear comfy shoes Saturday and Sunday. Both are big walking days.
  • Want more? Bar 54 (54th-floor rooftop by the hotel), Century 21 by the 9/11 Memorial, and The Nines piano bar are easy add-ons.
  • Want cheap show seats too? Wicked and Gatsby both run $40 to $55 lotteries and day-of rush, max 2 tickets per winner.
